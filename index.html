Word Yatra Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Word Yatra Android application

Effective date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Kotlin Knights operates the Word Yatra Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how Word Yatra accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

Word Yatra is an English-Hindi vocabulary and dictionary application with dictionary search, saved words, OCR word search, voice search, translation, and daily word reminders.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Word Yatra stores the following information locally on your Android device:

  • Search history.
  • Saved words and favorites.
  • Theme and display preferences.
  • Profile preferences such as display name and avatar.
  • Daily word reminder settings.
  • Local notification records.

Word Yatra does not require an account and does not maintain a server-side user profile for this local information.

3. Camera Permission and OCR

Word Yatra may request camera permission when you choose the Camera OCR Word Search feature.

Camera frames are used to recognize text and look up words. Word Yatra does not intentionally save camera frames or upload your photos to its own servers.

4. Microphone and Speech Recognition

Word Yatra may request microphone permission when you start Voice Search or Voice Translation.

Word Yatra does not intentionally save voice recordings. Android's speech-recognition service may process audio on the device or through its network service depending on the device, language, system configuration, and available speech model.

Speech-recognition processing may therefore be subject to the privacy terms of the Android or Google speech service provided by your device.

5. Google ML Kit and Translation

Word Yatra uses Google ML Kit for text recognition and translation features.

  • Translation models may be downloaded when required.
  • Translation can run on-device after the required model is available.
  • Text recognition is used to process text from the camera feature.

Google ML Kit services are provided by Google and may be subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

Word Yatra may display advertisements using Google AdMob to support application development and maintenance.

Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, region, and applicable law, AdMob may process information such as:

  • Google Advertising ID or another resettable advertising identifier.
  • IP address.
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address.
  • Device and application information.
  • Diagnostics and performance information.
  • Ad impressions and interaction information.

This information may be used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalized advertising where permitted.

You can manage advertising personalization or reset your Advertising ID from Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Word Yatra does not intentionally send your dictionary search text, saved definitions, camera OCR text, or voice input to advertising networks as app content.

7. Notifications

Word Yatra may request notification permission on Android 13 and later to deliver scheduled daily Word of the Day reminders.

Notification settings and local notification records are stored on your device.

8. Accounts and Analytics

  • No account creation is required.
  • No login, password, phone number, or email is required.
  • Word Yatra does not intentionally collect first-party behavioral analytics.
  • Word Yatra does not sell personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Word Yatra does not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or other personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us through the privacy contact below.

If Word Yatra is configured for a child-directed audience in Google Play Console, the applicable Google Play Families and Google AdMob requirements will be followed.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local search history, favorites, preferences, and notification records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application data.

You can remove local Word Yatra data by using Android Settings → Apps → Word Yatra → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the application.

Word Yatra does not maintain a user account or server-side profile that requires account deletion.

11. Data Security

Local application data is stored within Android application storage. Word Yatra requests permissions only when the related user-initiated feature is used.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
