1. Introduction

Overview

Moon Knight operates the Word Yatra Android application. This Privacy Policy outlines how Word Yatra accesses, utilizes, stores, and protects your information when you use our English-Hindi vocabulary, offline dictionary, and language discovery features.

Word Yatra delivers an editorial, distraction-free learning experience. By using Word Yatra, you agree to the privacy practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Local-First

All your learning progress and app customizations remain strictly inside your device's private local SQLite database and encrypted Android DataStore:

  • Search History: Word searches are indexed locally for quick review and can be cleared at any time in the History tab.
  • Saved Words & Favorites: Stored locally for instant offline revision.
  • Aesthetics & Preferences: Reading themes (Cream, Paper, Sepia, Dark, Obsidian), font choices, and playback speeds.
  • Scholar Profile: Custom display name and avatar illustrations chosen within the app.
  • Notification Records: Daily Word of the Day logs with automatic 30-day on-device cleanup.

Word Yatra does not require user registration and maintains zero server-side profiles for your lexical activity.

3. Camera Permission & OCR Word Lookup

Runtime Only

Word Yatra requests Camera access (android.permission.CAMERA) solely when you launch the Camera OCR Word Search feature to scan text from books or documents.

Camera video frames are processed completely in memory (RAM) via the Google ML Kit Vision SDK to extract word characters. Word Yatra never saves camera photos to storage and never uploads camera frames to external servers.

4. Microphone & Speech Recognition

Voice Search

Word Yatra requests Microphone access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) only when you initiate Voice Search or Voice Translation.

Word Yatra does not record, retain, or store your voice audio files. Speech recognition is handled directly by Android's native SpeechRecognizer framework, which processes audio either on-device or via Google's speech services depending on your device settings and installed speech models.

5. Google ML Kit On-Device Translation

On-Device AI

Word Yatra uses Google ML Kit for offline English-Hindi translation and optical character recognition:

  • Required language models are downloaded once via official Google Play services.
  • Once downloaded, translation inference runs locally on your device without transmitting text queries across the internet.

6. Advertising & Google AdMob

Ad-Supported

Word Yatra may display advertisements using Google AdMob to fund ongoing development and maintain free dictionary access. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC.

Depending on your device configuration, Google Play services settings, and applicable laws, Google AdMob may process:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or resettable advertising identifiers.
  • Device IP address (used for approximate regional geolocation).
  • Device specifications, operating system version, and performance diagnostics.
  • Ad interaction and impression metrics.

Privacy Safeguard: Word Yatra does not share your dictionary searches, reading history, saved definitions, OCR text, or voice input with advertising networks.

You can manage ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID anytime by opening Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Notifications

Daily Word

On Android 13 (API level 33) and above, Word Yatra may request POST_NOTIFICATIONS to deliver scheduled daily "Word of the Day" reminders via on-device Android WorkManager. You can enable, disable, or adjust notification timings anytime inside the Profile → Notification Settings screen.

8. Accounts, Telemetry & Open Licenses

Zero Profile
  • No User Accounts: No sign-in, email address, password, or phone number required.
  • No First-Party Telemetry: We do not track individual user journeys, screen heatmaps, or keystrokes.
  • No Sale of Data: Word Yatra will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.
  • Open Lexicon Data: Word definitions are derived from Wiktionary contributors under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0) and GNU Free Documentation License (GFDL).

9. Children's Privacy

Play Families

Word Yatra is a general-audience educational vocabulary reference. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe personal information has inadvertently been provided by a minor, please reach out to us at our contact email so we can promptly address the inquiry.

10. Data Retention & Full Deletion

User Control

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, you have complete ownership and control over data deletion:

  • In-App Clear: Tap the trash icon in the History screen to instantly purge all stored search queries.
  • Full System Wipe: Open Android Settings → Apps → Word Yatra → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage. This permanently erases all local database records, saved words, and user preferences.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the app automatically deletes all private app storage directories.

11. Security & Policy Updates

Protection

We apply standard Android security sandboxing to protect local databases. We may revise this policy periodically to reflect feature additions or regulatory standards. Updates are posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.
